Questions parents ask.

Do I need my child's coach or team to sign up?

No. Moma Stats is your family's own record of your player. There's no team account, and nothing goes to the team or the coach unless you choose to share it.

Do I have to download it from the App Store?

No. Open momastats.com on your phone and add it to your home screen; it then opens like any other app, on iPhone, iPad and Android. We show you how, step by step, when you sign up.

What if the gym has no signal?

Keep tapping. Every stat saves on your phone right away and sends itself as soon as you're back online. You can even start a new game with no signal.

Do you store my videos?

Never. Your videos stay on your phone. When you want to share one, it goes straight from your phone to whoever you send it to. You can also paste a link to a video you posted on your own YouTube.

Which stats can I keep?

Points, made and missed 2s, 3s and free throws, where each shot came from, offensive and defensive rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers and fouls. Moma Stats works out the percentages and season averages for you.

Can two parents keep stats?

Yes. Invite your partner, or a grandparent, at no extra cost. One phone keeps the stats at each game so nothing is counted twice, and everyone else can watch live.

I have more than one child playing. Is that extra?

No. Up to 5 players are included, each with their own seasons and history.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from Account in the menu; it takes about a minute. Cancel during the free trial and you're never charged.
